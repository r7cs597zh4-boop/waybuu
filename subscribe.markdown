Want new posts to come to you instead of checking back? You can follow this blog with an **RSS reader**, a free app that collects posts from the sites you like in one place.

**How to subscribe**

1. Get a reader app. Free options include [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com) (iPhone, iPad and Mac), [Feedly](https://feedly.com) and [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com).
2. Add this feed address in the app: `https://waybuu.com/feed.xml`
3. New posts will show up there automatically.
